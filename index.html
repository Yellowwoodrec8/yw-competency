Position Based Competency Guide

Select your role for step-by-step instructions.

Complete your self-assessment before your first training session. Your trainer cannot add their scores until you sign.
1
Log in and go to My Competencies
You'll see your Position Based Competency card below your general competencies — it has a blue left border.
2
Open the form and fill in your Date of Hire
Click Continue on the Position Based Competency card. Fill in your date of hire at the top.
3
Rate yourself on each skill item
Use the dropdown next to each item: 1 = Proficient, 2 = Limited Experience, 3 = Need Instruction. Be honest — this helps your trainer know where to focus.
4
Sign your self-assessment
Scroll to the bottom, click "Click to sign self-assessment", enter your name and draw your signature. Once signed your scores lock and your trainer can begin adding sessions.
The new hire must sign their self-assessment before you can add a session. If the button is not showing, ask them to complete and sign their self-assessment first.

Option A — Sign off together (new hire present)

1
Have the new hire open their form
The new hire logs in and opens their Position Based Competency form. Work through the skill items together during the shift.
2
Score the items you covered today
For each item you observed or trained on, enter your evaluation score (1/2/3) and method (1-4) using the dropdowns. Only score items you actually covered.
3
Click Add Trainer Session
Enter your full name, select the session date, draw your signature and click Sign Off.

Option B — Sign off remotely (new hire not present)

Use this option when the new hire is not available. The new hire generates a one-time code and shares it with you. No login required.
1
Ask the new hire to generate a code
The new hire opens their Position Based Competency form, then clicks Generate Trainer Code. A 6-digit code appears — they share it with you by text or in person.
2
Go to the app and click the sign-off code link
Open yellowwoodrec8.github.io/yw-competency in your browser. On the login page, click “I have a trainer sign-off code” at the bottom.
3
Enter the 6-digit code
Type in the code the new hire gave you and click Access Form. You'll go directly to their floor competency form.
4
Score items and sign your session
Enter your evaluation scores and method for the items you covered, then click Add Trainer Session, enter your name, select the date, draw your signature, and click Sign Off.
The code expires after 7 days and can only be used once. If it has expired, ask the new hire to generate a new one.
1
Track status from the dashboard
The staff table shows a Floor Training column. Badges update automatically: Not Started, Self-Assess Done, Training (X sessions), Complete.
2
Click the badge to view the form
Clicking any floor training badge opens a read-only view of that employee's form — all self-scores, every session, who signed, and the dates.
3
Mark as complete when training is done
The new hire or any trainer can click Mark as Complete once all sessions are finished. The badge turns green on your dashboard.
